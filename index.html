Welcome to Nano Knights

We're kicking off our third season in FTC: BIOBUZZ, presented by RTX. It's a biodiversity-themed challenge built around a new scoring element called Pollen, and we're starting from a blank sheet of paper to design and build a robot for it.

Six members, one robot, zero shortcuts. We design, build, program, and debug everything ourselves, carrying two straight 8th-place finishes in the Goddard League into a season where we're aiming for the top 5.
