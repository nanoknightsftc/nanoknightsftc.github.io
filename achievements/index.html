Achievements & Timeline

2026-2027 Season - IN PROGRESS

BIOBUZZ Season Kickoff

Kicked off our third FTC season with BIOBUZZ, presented by RTX: a biodiversity-themed challenge built around a new scoring element called Pollen. We're back in the shop applying everything we learned from two straight 8th-place finishes in the Goddard League toward our most ambitious robot yet.

2025-2026 Season - COMPLETED

DECODE Season - 8th Place Finish

Finished our second FTC season 8th in the Goddard League, matching our first-year result in the DECODE challenge. We built on lessons from our first season and grew in technical skills, strategy, and collaboration.

2024-2025 Season - COMPLETED

INTO THE DEEP - First Competition Season

Our inaugural season in FTC. Competed in the Into The Deep challenge and achieved 8th place in the Goddard League, surpassing our initial expectations as a first-year team. This season established our foundation and proved that our team could compete effectively at the FTC level.

2024

Team Formation

Nano Knights FTC Team 26147 was established with five dedicated members passionate about robotics, engineering, and STEM education. We began building our team structure, learning FTC requirements, and preparing for our first competitive season.

Key Accomplishments

Consistent Performance: Finished 8th in the Goddard League in both our Into The Deep (2024-2025) and DECODE (2025-2026) seasons.

Two Complete Seasons: Successfully competed in two full FTC seasons, building experience and expertise with each challenge.

Team Development: Built a cohesive six-member team with clear roles and responsibilities, establishing effective communication and collaboration practices.

Technical Growth: Developed proficiency in robot design, CAD modeling, programming, and competition strategy over two seasons, with continuous improvement in our engineering process.

Community Engagement: Established relationships with sponsors and built our presence in the local STEM community.

Looking Ahead

Our third season is underway. Two 8th-place finishes in the Goddard League gave us a solid foundation, and this year we're focused on moving up the rankings. Our goals for BIOBUZZ are to break into the top 5, earn recognition for our engineering, and keep growing as individuals and as a team.
